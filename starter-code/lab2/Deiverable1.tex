\section{Published and Subscribed Topics}

\subsection*{How I Checked}

\placeholder{I used \cmd{ros2 node info <topic\_name>}.} \\
\\
\cmd{/av1broadcaster} publishes the \cmd{av1} frame on
\cmd{/tf\_static}. \\
\cmd{/av2broadcaster} publishes the \cmd{av2} frame on \cmd{/tf\_static}. \\
\cmd{/plots\_publisher\_node} publishes visualization markers on \cmd{/visuals}. \\
\cmd{/rviz2\_node} subscribes to \cmd{/visuals} and displays the drone meshes. \\

\subsection*{Frame Publishers}

\paragraph{Question.}
What nodes are responsible for publishing the \cmd{av1} and \cmd{av2} frames?

\paragraph{Answer.}
av1broadcaster and av2broadcaster nodes are responsible to publish transforms for \\
av1 and av2. 
\subsection*{rViz Drone Mesh Topic}

\paragraph{Question.}
Which topic causes rViz to plot the drone meshes?

\paragraph{Answer.}
rViz uses $visuals$ topic to plot the drone meshes.
